---
title: Addressing social needs
tags: documented social needs, social isolation, housing, transportation, unemployed, social work, community resources, caregiver
references: CMS Accountable Health Communities Health-Related Social Needs Screening Tool
---
Social circumstances strongly influence whether a discharge plan can actually be followed. Screen for transportation, housing stability, food security, utilities, and social isolation, and act on what is found: social work referral, transportation assistance for follow-up visits, connection to community organizations, and engagement of family or other caregivers with the patient's permission. Document needs and referrals so the whole care team can follow up on them.
