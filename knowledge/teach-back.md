---
title: Patient education with teach-back
tags: patient education, health literacy, teach-back, discharge instructions, caregiver, length of stay
references: AHRQ Health Literacy Universal Precautions Toolkit (Teach-Back tool)
---
Discharge instructions are frequently misunderstood. Use plain language, focus on the few most important actions, and ask the patient (and caregiver) to explain the plan back in their own words: which medications to take, which warning signs matter, and whom to call. Provide written materials at an appropriate reading level and in the patient's preferred language. Teach-back checks the clarity of the explanation, not the patient's intelligence.
