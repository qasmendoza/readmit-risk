The score estimates the probability of an acute hospital readmission within 30 days of discharge. Tiers reflect care-management capacity: "high" marks roughly the top 10% of discharges by predicted risk. The score is decision support to prioritize outreach, not a diagnosis, and must not replace clinical judgment. In this project it was developed and evaluated only on synthetic data, does not apply the CMS planned-readmission algorithm, and has not been validated on real patients.
