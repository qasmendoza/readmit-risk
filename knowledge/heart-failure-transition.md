---
title: Heart failure transitional care
tags: congestive heart failure, chf, diuretic, sodium, daily weights, fluid, cardiology
references: 2022 AHA/ACC/HFSA Guideline for the Management of Heart Failure (Heidenreich PA et al.)
---
Heart failure is one of the most common causes of readmission. Core transition practices include teaching daily weight checks and symptom monitoring with a clear plan for when to call (for example, a rapid weight gain threshold set by the care team), reviewing diuretic and guideline-directed medication adherence, and an early follow-up visit, ideally within about a week. Low sodium levels or worsening kidney function may warrant closer laboratory follow-up as directed by clinicians. Referral to a heart failure clinic, cardiac rehabilitation, or remote monitoring program can be considered for high-risk patients.
