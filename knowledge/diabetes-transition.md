---
title: Diabetes care after discharge
tags: diabetes with complications, hba1c, glucose, insulin, hypoglycemia, endocrinology
references: American Diabetes Association, Standards of Care in Diabetes (Diabetes Care in the Hospital)
---
Glucose-lowering regimens often change during a hospital stay, which raises the risk of hypoglycemia or hyperglycemia at home. Confirm the patient has a glucose monitoring plan and the supplies to follow it, and review the signs of low blood sugar and what to do about them, especially when insulin is involved. Teach sick-day guidance as provided by the care team, and schedule follow-up to review the regimen and recent HbA1c. Complications such as kidney disease or neuropathy may need coordinated specialist follow-up.
