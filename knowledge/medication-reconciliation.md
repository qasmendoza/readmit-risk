---
title: Medication reconciliation and polypharmacy
tags: polypharmacy, distinct medications prescribed, medication changes, insulin, diuretic, anticoagulant, pharmacist
references: The Joint Commission National Patient Safety Goal NPSG.03.06.01; AHRQ MATCH Toolkit for Medication Reconciliation
---
Patients taking many medications face a higher risk of adverse drug events after discharge. Compare the pre-admission, inpatient, and discharge medication lists and resolve every discrepancy, clearly marking which medications are new, changed, or stopped. Pharmacist-led reconciliation and a review of high-risk classes (insulin, anticoagulants, diuretics, opioids) are common safeguards. Give the patient one updated list, check they can obtain and afford new prescriptions, and confirm understanding with teach-back. Any change to a regimen is a clinical decision for the prescribing team.
