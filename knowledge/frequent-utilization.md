---
title: Frequent hospital and emergency department use
tags: emergency department visits, hospitalizations in the prior 12 months, frequent utilization, care management, care navigation
references: AHRQ, Designing and Delivering Whole-Person Transitional Care (Hospital Guide to Reducing Medicaid Readmissions); CMS Hospital Readmissions Reduction Program
---
Repeated emergency visits or admissions usually signal unmet needs: limited access to primary care, poorly controlled chronic disease, behavioral health conditions, or social barriers. Care-management enrollment with a named care manager, a reliable after-hours contact, and a plan built around the patient's own reasons for seeking care can reduce avoidable returns. Make sure the patient has an established primary care home and knows which concerns can be handled by phone, telehealth, or urgent care.
