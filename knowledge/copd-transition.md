---
title: COPD transitional care
tags: chronic pulmonary disease, copd, emphysema, chronic bronchitis, inhaler, exacerbation, pulmonary rehabilitation
references: Global Initiative for Chronic Obstructive Lung Disease (GOLD) Report
---
After a COPD exacerbation, check inhaler technique and that the patient has every prescribed inhaler, since incorrect use is common. Provide a written action plan describing early warning signs and what to do. Offer smoking cessation support where relevant, and consider referral to pulmonary rehabilitation, which is recommended after hospitalization for an exacerbation. Arrange follow-up to review symptoms and confirm the maintenance regimen chosen by the care team.
