---
title: Timely post-discharge follow-up
tags: follow-up appointment, high risk, hospitalizations in the prior 12 months, length of stay, transitional care, primary care
references: AHRQ Re-Engineered Discharge (RED) Toolkit; Hernandez AF et al. JAMA 2010;303(17):1716-1722
---
Schedule the first outpatient visit before the patient leaves the hospital rather than asking them to arrange it later. Programs commonly target a visit within 7 days of discharge for high-risk patients and within 14 days for others; earlier follow-up has been associated with fewer readmissions in several studies, particularly in heart failure. Confirm the patient can get to the appointment, send the discharge summary to the receiving clinician before the visit, and make a follow-up phone call within 2 to 3 days to check symptoms, medications, and questions.
