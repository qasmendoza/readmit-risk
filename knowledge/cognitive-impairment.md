---
title: Cognitive impairment and caregiver engagement
tags: dementia, cognitive impairment, caregiver, home health, medication management, safety
references: National Institute on Aging, caregiving resources
---
Patients with dementia or other cognitive impairment may not be able to follow a complex discharge plan on their own. With the patient's permission, involve caregivers in discharge planning and education, simplify medication schedules where the care team agrees, and consider pill organizers or blister packs. Evaluate home safety and the need for home health services, and confirm who will accompany the patient to follow-up appointments.
