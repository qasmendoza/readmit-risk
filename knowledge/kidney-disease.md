---
title: Chronic kidney disease considerations
tags: moderate or severe chronic kidney disease, renal disease, egfr, creatinine, nephrology, medication dosing
references: KDIGO Clinical Practice Guideline for the Evaluation and Management of Chronic Kidney Disease
---
Reduced kidney function affects how many drugs are cleared, so discharge medications should be reviewed for kidney-appropriate dosing by the clinical team. Patients are commonly advised to avoid nephrotoxic over-the-counter products such as NSAIDs unless a clinician approves them. When diuretics or blood-pressure medications change, follow-up laboratory checks of creatinine and potassium are often arranged. Coordinate with nephrology for patients with advanced disease or on dialysis.
